# Adjudicate and backfill

Identify the exact claim, assumptions, evidence and the decision it would change. Reproduce the decisive proof step, calculation, source check or counterexample independently. A student's file, log or confidence is evidence to inspect, not authority to accept.

Record accept, narrow, reject or unresolved with the reason and the evidence path. Propagate an accepted correction into every affected conclusion, current state and user-facing deliverable; keep the original report and your verdict distinct. Use `templates/adjudication.md` when a written verdict helps later recovery.

只对会影响判断或后续工作的分歧做复核；低影响的分歧可以记为未决，不启动全套流程。重复出现、代价高的错误才留下回归检查。
